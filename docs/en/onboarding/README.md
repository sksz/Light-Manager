# Onboarding — the first thirty minutes

**A path, not a reference.** The guide is something you come back to; onboarding
is walked once. So nothing is explained here from scratch — onboarding leads you
and **points** at the [guide](../guide/README.md).

Who it is for: you know PHP, you do not know this project. At the end of the
path you have **a change of your own with a green quality gate** — without
asking the author and without reading anything beyond these five pages.

Five stops, each with a one-sentence "how you know you are done", mirroring
[../../pl/onboarding/](../../pl/onboarding/README.md):

| File | Stop | Time |
|---|---|---|
| [1. Environment](01-environment.md) | `git clone`, `make check-env`, `make install` — and a map of what is optional. | 5 min |
| [2. Running](02-running.md) | `make run`, the three frame-drawing paths, and what happens at startup. | 5 min |
| [3. Looking around](03-looking-around.md) | One tour: a directory, `F12`, `Tab` in the command window, `F9`, a module shortcut. | 5 min |
| [4. First change](04-first-change.md) | The exercise: a module with a query, a starting file and a solution in [`examples/`](../../../examples/zadanie-kwerenda/). | 10 min |
| [5. Where next](05-where-next.md) | The documentation map in one paragraph and three questions with answers. | 5 min |

**No stop points at a document longer than a page** — and where a chapter of the
guide is long, the link aims at the section itself. Onboarding that tells you at
step three to read two thousand lines of architecture is not onboarding, it is
a reading list.

Onboarding is verified by **someone other than its author walking the path** —
not by reading it.
